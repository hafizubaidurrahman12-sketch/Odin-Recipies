Step by Step instructions to make Pasta.

  1. Bring a large pot of water to a rolling boil
  2. add plenty of salt until it tastes like the ocean
  3. add the pasta
  4. Cook until al dente (firm to the bite)
  5. drain and reserve a half-cup of pasta water
  6. Heat olive oil in a pan over medium heat, then add the chopped onions and sliced garlic. Sauté until golden and soft.
  7. Pour in the tomato puree, red chili flakes, mixed herbs, salt, and black pepper.
  8. Cook the sauce for 8 to 10 minutes until thick and fragrant.
  9. Add the cooked pasta and a splash of the reserved starchy water into the tomato sauce.
  10. Toss well over heat so the sauce coats every piece.
  11. Plate the hot pasta and garnish generously with fresh parmesan cheese.
